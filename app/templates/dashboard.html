How AI Face Attendance Works

  1. Student Registration: Register a student with their Roll Number, Name, Class, and take/upload their face photo. The photo is compressed and saved in the database in Base64 format.
  2. Live Face Matching: When marking attendance, the webcam captures the student's live face in real-time.
  3. Open-Source AI Verification: The live face is matched against stored Base64 images using the open-source DeepFace (Facenet) model.
  4. Automatic Attendance: Once verified, attendance is recorded with exact timestamp and confidence score. Duplicate entries on the same day are automatically prevented.
